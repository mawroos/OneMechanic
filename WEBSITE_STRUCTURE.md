# OneMechanic Website Structure

## Website Overview

The OneMechanic website is a professional single-page car service website designed for mobile mechanics who provide services at customers' homes.

## Page Sections

### 1. Header/Navigation Bar
- **Logo**: OneMechanic with car icon
- **Navigation Menu**: Home, About, Services, Booking, Contact
- **Top Bar**: Contact info (phone, hours) and social media links
- **CTA Button**: "Get A Quote" 

### 2. Hero Section (Carousel)
- **Slide 1**: "Professional Mobile Mechanic Service" 
  - Tagline: "Car Service at Your Home"
  - CTA: "Book Now"
- **Slide 2**: "Quality Service You Can Trust"
  - Tagline: "Expert Auto Repair"
  - CTA: "Book Now"

### 3. Services Overview
Three highlighted features:
- Quality Servicing
- Expert Workers  
- Modern Equipment

### 4. About Section
- Company introduction
- 5+ years experience badge
- Three key points:
  1. Professional & Expert
  2. Quality Servicing Center
  3. Award Winning Workers
- CTA: "Book An Appointment"

### 5. Statistics Section
Counter showcase:
- 1234 Services Completed
- 5 Expert Technicians
- 1000 Satisfied Clients
- 500 Mobile Units

### 6. Detailed Services
Tabbed service display:
- Diagnostic Service
- Engine Servicing
- Tires Replacement
- Oil Changing

Each service includes:
- Service image
- Description
- Feature checklist
- "Book Now" button

### 7. Booking Form
Two-column layout:
- Left: Call to action text
- Right: Booking form with fields:
  - Name
  - Email
  - Service selection
  - Date picker
  - Special requests
  - Submit button

### 8. Testimonials
Carousel with customer reviews:
- 4 testimonials
- Customer photos
- Names and professions
- Review text

### 9. Contact Section
- Contact information boxes (Mobile Service, Phone, Email)
- Google Maps integration
- Contact form

### 10. Footer
Four columns:
- Address & social media
- Opening hours
- Quick service links
- Newsletter signup

## Design Features

- **Responsive**: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Modern**: Bootstrap 5 framework
- **Animated**: Smooth scroll animations (WOW.js)
- **Interactive**: Carousels, tabbed content, forms
- **Professional**: Clean red (#D81324) and blue (#0B2154) color scheme

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3 (Bootstrap 5)
- JavaScript (jQuery)
- Font Awesome icons
- Google Fonts (Barlow, Ubuntu)
- Owl Carousel
- WOW.js animations
- Tempus Dominus (date picker)
